## Limits and next gate

The digest change does **not** make filesystem writes atomic. CLI write,
arbitrate, triage and normalize still use direct write paths; T02 owns contention,
semantic verification, durable receipts, history and interruption recovery.
`init` refuses any existing content and does not recover a partially completed
bootstrap. Identity checks do not certify authenticity. The CLI's frozen/symlink
checks do not protect against external editors or races during a command.
Accomplishment stubs still require their existing source/evidence completion.

Live-KB session capture is unavailable: ARBITER_DATA resolves to the frozen
in-repo corpus and `../arbiter-data` is absent. No substitute live KB was created.
