## Limits and successor

Capture is a sequence of durable writes under one cooperating lock, not an atomic
multi-file transaction. A failure can leave updated task metadata with an old or
missing checkpoint; refresh, inspect/recover journals and explicitly re-observe
inputs before retry. It never discards an existing checkpoint or retained history.
No external-editor or network-filesystem guarantee. Preview basis is deliberately
conservative: unrelated current-corpus edits can invalidate it.

Normal packets include the complete installed checkpoint rules, making even a
compact packet approach 10 KiB; warnings and visible overrides are deliberate.
Offline full-contract packets generally require an override and remain planning
snapshots until live rechecks. Repo/external content is not fetched or bundled;
caller observations do not establish current authority. Known-token/credential
checks cannot recognize arbitrary secrets or paraphrased private facts.
